# WooCommerce Minimum Profit Margin

A WooCommerce store can run profitable promotions only if discounts stay above the store's required margin floor.

Gross margin is `(revenue - cost) / revenue`. If a product costs **$80** and the required margin is **20%**, the minimum acceptable revenue is **$100**.

Margin Guard is designed to enforce that kind of floor during coupon calculations instead of relying only on cart-spend restrictions.
