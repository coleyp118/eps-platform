# WooCommerce Storewide Coupon Profit Protection

Storewide coupons are risky when a catalog contains products with very different margins. The same percentage discount can be harmless on one item and destroy profit on another.

Margin Guard is designed to cap coupon discounts against cost-aware margin floors instead of treating every product as if it had the same economics.
