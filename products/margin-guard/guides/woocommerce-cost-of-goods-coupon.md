# WooCommerce Cost of Goods Coupon Control

Product cost changes the real value of a coupon. A 20% discount can be safe on a high-margin item and unprofitable on a low-margin item.

Margin Guard uses product cost plus a required gross-margin floor to determine how much coupon discount can safely be applied.

Margin Guard Pro v1.2 can use WooCommerce's native Cost of Goods data when available, with a fallback for older setups.
