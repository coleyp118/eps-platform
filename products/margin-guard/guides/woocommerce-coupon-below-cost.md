# WooCommerce Coupon Below Cost

WooCommerce coupons can be restricted by spend, products, categories, users and usage limits, but a store may still need a separate rule based on **product cost and required gross margin**.

Example: if a product costs **$80** and the store requires a **20% gross margin**, revenue must stay at **$100 or more**. A coupon that would push the item below $100 crosses that margin floor.
