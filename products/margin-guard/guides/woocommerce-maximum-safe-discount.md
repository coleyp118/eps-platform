# WooCommerce Maximum Safe Discount

The maximum safe discount depends on product price, product cost, and the gross margin the store wants to preserve.

For example, with a $100 price, $55 product cost, and a 25% minimum gross margin, the maximum discount can be calculated before a promotion goes live. Margin Guard automates that protection during WooCommerce coupon calculations.
