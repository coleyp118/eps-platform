# Margin Guard for WooCommerce

**WooCommerce minimum profit margin and coupon protection.**

Margin Guard prevents coupon discounts from pushing products below a configured minimum gross margin.

If a product costs $80 and the store requires a 20% gross margin, the product needs at least $100 in revenue. Margin Guard caps the coupon discount before the item falls below that floor.
